Our Story

More than a cafe –
a home away from home

Cafe Royal was born from a simple belief: that great coffee, lovingly made food and a genuinely warm welcome can brighten anyone's day.

We source our single-origin beans with care, roasting them in small batches every morning so each cup is alive with flavour. Our kitchen team bakes fresh pastries, cakes and patisserie daily, using seasonal, locally sourced ingredients wherever possible.

From a solo espresso at the bar to a long, leisurely dinner with friends – Cafe Royal is your space. Pull up a chair, stay a while.
