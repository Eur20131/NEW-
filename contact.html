Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes! We always keep a number of tables free for walk-in guests. However, booking is recommended, especially on weekends and evenings.

Is there a cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours' notice for cancellations. For groups of 6+, 48 hours' notice is required. No-shows may be charged a £5 per person fee.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Our menu clearly marks vegan (V), vegetarian (VG) and gluten-free (GF) options. Please inform us of any allergies when booking.

Can I hire the cafe for a private event?

Yes, we offer private hire for groups. Please contact us via phone or email to discuss your requirements and receive a tailored quote.

Is Cafe Royal child-friendly?

Absolutely! We have a dedicated kids' menu, high chairs available and a warm, welcoming atmosphere for families of all sizes.

Do you have free Wi-Fi?

Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the cafe. Ask your server for the password.
